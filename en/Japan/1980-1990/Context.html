Japan: Context (1980-1990)

Geopolitical Dynamics and International Relationships

  1. Asian Countries

[China]

Relations with China

1982: The First Textbook Issue

In 1982, Japanese media outlets simultaneously reported on the results of the high school textbook screening process for textbooks to be used beginning in 1983. According to these reports, the Ministry of Education had instructed publishers to revise phrases such as “invasion” of China by Japan in the 1930s to terms like “advance” or “military action,” and had also pressured them to tone down the brutality and Japanese military responsibility regarding the “Nanjing Massacre” (p. 190).

Although there was considerable domestic criticism of these decisions, the Chinese government grew increasingly opposed to what it saw as Japan’s official endorsement of “reactionary” textbook content and strongly demanded revisions.

The Ministry of Education, which emphasized the independence of the textbook screening process, found itself at odds with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, which prioritized Japan’s diplomatic relationship with China. Prime Minister Zenko Suzuki’s stance was closer to that of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (p. 190).

In August, Chief Cabinet Secretary Kiichi Miyazawa promised that the problematic textbook passages would be revised. He also stated that Japan would ensure that the spirit of the Japan-China Joint Declaration—acknowledging Japan’s responsibility and expressing remorse for the significant damage inflicted upon China during the war—would be “adequately reflected” in textbook descriptions (p. 191).

Following Miyazawa’s statement, a new guideline was introduced into the textbook screening process: that “adequate consideration must be given, from the standpoint of fostering understanding and international harmony, to the treatment of modern and contemporary historical events involving neighboring Asian countries” (the so-called “neighboring countries clause”) (p. 191).

Since the signing of the peace treaty in 1978, the Chinese government had been careful to avoid making historical issues a diplomatic problem with Japan. However, during this period, China launched an anti-Japan campaign, which was driven by several factors (p. 192):

  • To demonstrate an independent foreign policy stance both domestically and internationally.

  • To promote the “patriotic united front” as a new ideological pillar for national unity to replace communist ideology, emphasizing national unity and the legitimacy of the Communist Party by drawing on the history of victory in the war of resistance against Japan.

  • The visit of a Liberal Democratic Party (LDP) delegation to Taiwan further inflamed the Chinese government’s anti-Japan campaign.

[China]

中国との関係

1982年、第一次教科書問題

1982年、日本の報道機関は、1983年から使用される高校教科書検定の結果を一斉に報じた。その内容は、文部省が日本史や世界史の教科書で1930年代の日本による中国への「侵略」ということばを「進出」や「侵攻」へ書き換えさせたり、「南京虐殺」についてその残虐性や日本軍の責任を薄める記述を出版各社に求めたというものだった。（190)

こうした決定について日本国内でも批判的な声が多かったが、中国政府はに「復古調」の教科書を容認・推奨する日本政府に対する反発を強め、内容の是正を強く求めた。

この問題に対しては、検定制度の自主性を重んじる文科省と中国との関係を重視する外務省との間に溝が生じていたが、鈴木善幸首相館の立場は外務省の立場に近かった（190)

また日本政府は8月、宮沢喜一官房長官は問題の記述について改定することを約束した。また、戦争を通じて日本が中国に大きな損害を与えたことの責任と反省を謳った日中共同声明の精神が教科書の記述に「十分実現するよう配慮する」旨を述べた（191)

宮沢の談話を受け、以後、歴史教科書検定におては「近隣アジア諸国との間の近現代の歴史的事変の扱いに国家理解と国際協調の見地から必要な配慮がなされていること」という規定（いわゆる「近隣諸国条項」）がも儲けられることになった（191)

1978年の平和条約締結以降、歴史問題が日中間の外交問題になることを慎重に避けてきた中国政府が、この時期に反日キャンペーンを繰り広げてきたことについては、幾つかの要因がある。（192)

  1. 独立自主の対外政策を国内外に示すため

  2. 共産主義イデオロギーに変わるあらたな民族統合の思想的支柱として「愛国統一戦線」を打ち出し、抗日戦争勝利の歴史から民族の団結や共産党の正当性を訴えようとした。

  3. 台湾との日本との関係で自民党団の台湾訪問が中国政府反日キャンペーンに油をそそいた。

1985: Yasukuni Shrine Official Visit Issue

In 1975, Prime Minister Takeo Miki became the first postwar prime minister to visit Yasukuni Shrine on August 15, the anniversary of the end of World War II. At that time, Miki emphasized that he was visiting in a “private” capacity. Since then, successive prime ministers began visiting the shrine on August 15 as well, though always in a “private” capacity.

According to the 1955 unified government interpretation, an official visit to Yasukuni Shrine by public officials—including the Prime Minister—could potentially violate the Constitution, and therefore such visits by Cabinet members were to be refrained from (p. 198).

However, on August 15, 1985, Prime Minister Yasuhiro Nakasone visited Yasukuni Shrine in an official capacity, which caused considerable controversy both domestically and internationally.

Behind Nakasone’s decision were requests from the Bereaved Families Association, a resolution by the LDP’s General Council, and resolutions from local assemblies. Pressure in favor of an official prime ministerial visit had steadily escalated.

A panel of experts also suggested that depending on how the visit was conducted, an official visit by the prime minister could be constitutionally permissible (p. 199).

In addition, efforts were made to ease concerns in China: in July 1985, Japanese lawmakers were sent to China to attempt persuasion. However, the Chinese side strongly objected to the enshrinement of Class-A war criminals, especially Hideki Tojo. Though no understanding was reached with China, the Japanese government tried to maintain diplomatic courtesy by notifying the Chinese government in advance of the official visit (p. 199).

Thus, on August 15, Nakasone, accompanied by 16 Cabinet members, made an official visit to Yasukuni Shrine. To downplay the religious aspect, no religious rites were performed; the visit was conducted in a simplified manner. However, he signed the shrine’s guestbook as “Prime Minister” and the offering of flowers was paid for with public funds.

Chief Cabinet Secretary Fujinami issued a statement the day before the visit, expressing the government’s view that this type of visit, “lacking religious significance,” did not constitute “religious activity prohibited by the Constitution” from the standpoint of “social convention” (p. 199).

1985年 靖国神社公式参拝問題

1975年三木武夫が戦後の首相として初めて終戦記念日の8月15日に参拝した。その際、三木は「私人」として参拝したことを強調した。以後、終戦記念日にも首相が参拝するようになったが、それらは全て「私人」の肩書きで行われた。

1955年の政府統一見解では、公人としての靖国神社参拝は憲法違反の疑いがあるので、内閣総理大臣も含めた国務大臣による参拝は差し控える、というものであった(198)

1985年8月15日、中曽根康弘首相が「公人」として靖国神社に参拝した。この参拝は国内外で大きな波紋を読んだ。

中曽根が公式参拝に踏み切った背景には、遺族会からの要請、自民党総務会の決議、地方議会の決議などがあった。様々な首相の公式参拝を期待する圧力がエスカレート。

参拝のやり方如何によっては、憲法解釈上首相の公式参拝は可能とする見方が有識者会議で出された（199)

さらに中国に対しても、1985年7月に国会議員を中国に派遣し、説得工作を試みた。しかし、中国側はA級戦犯、とくに東條英機の合祀を問題視した。結局中国からの理解は得られなかったが、公式参拝を事前に伝えることで、中国政府に仁義を切るという体裁を整えた（199)

こうして8月15日、16名の閣僚を引き連れて中曽根は靖国神社を公式参拝した。宗教色を薄めるために、宗教的な儀礼は行わず、簡略化して参拝した。一方で、「内閣総理大臣」時調子、供花料は「公費」で拠出した。内閣官房長官藤浪は、参拝の前日に談話を発表し、こうした「宗教的意義を有しない」参拝方式であれば「社会通念上、憲法が禁止する宗教的活動」に該当しないとの政府見解を示した（199)

China’s criticism of Japan was generally restrained. It is said that reformist leaders close to Nakasone—Hu Yaobang, Zhao Ziyang, and Deng Xiaoping—hoped for an early resolution to the situation. However, anti-Japanese demonstrations broke out one after another inside China, spreading to cities such as Beijing, Xi’an, Yan’an, and Chengdu in Sichuan Province (p. 199).

Some of these protests also reflected public dissatisfaction with the government, stemming from widening social disparities under China’s reform and opening-up policies (p. 199).

Although Japan continued its efforts to explain the situation to China, it was unable to gain Beijing’s understanding. As a result, Nakasone abandoned plans to visit Yasukuni Shrine the following year. From that point on, he did not visit Yasukuni again during his tenure as prime minister (p. 200).

One reason Nakasone suspended his official visits was his close personal relationship with Hu Yaobang; he was concerned about the potential negative impact on Hu within China if he were to visit the shrine. Another factor was the failure of efforts to separate the enshrinement of Class-A war criminals. After it became public in 1978 that Class-A war criminals had been enshrined at Yasukuni, there was a strong domestic and international backlash, and Emperor Showa also stopped his visits to the shrine (p. 201).

1986: The Second Textbook Issue

In May 1986, New Edition Japanese History, a high school history textbook compiled by the conservative group National Conference to Defend Japan, passed the final stage of the textbook screening process. The National Conference to Defend Japan, founded in 1981, was a conservative political and advocacy group. The textbook included numerous passages that justified Japan’s foreign aggression or emphasized Japan’s war victimization, and was strongly nationalistic in tone.

Naturally, both China and South Korea demanded revisions to the content. The Japanese government requested that the publisher withdraw the application to sell the textbook. However, the Ministry of Education strongly resisted what it saw as political interference to halt publication.

Compared to the 1982 textbook issue, the 1986 Second Textbook Issue was resolved relatively quickly without escalating into a major diplomatic incident. One key reason was that Nakasone and Hu maintained a close personal relationship and neither wished to see Japan-China relations deteriorate. Both leaders worked to manage the situation constructively (p. 203).

1989: Tiananmen Incident

In response to the Tiananmen Incident, the Japanese government emphasized that because of Japan’s own history of aggression in China, its relationship with China was different from that of other major powers. However, while other leading nations imposed restrictions on their economic relations with China, Japan did not implement such measures, leading to growing criticism that Japan was placing business interests ahead of human rights. In response, the Japanese government eventually announced a suspension of yen loans to China and froze ministerial-level contact with the Chinese government (p. 227).

Reference

Akiaki Miyashita, Handbook of Postwar Japanese Diplomatic History: From the Peace Treaty with Japan to the Secret Agreement Issue, Minerva Shobō, 2017.

中国の対日批判は全体として抑制的であった。中曽根と親しい関係であった胡耀邦や趙紫陽、鄧小平らは改革派は事態の早期収集を望んでいたと言われている。しかし、中国国内で反日でもが相次ぎ、北京、西安市、延安市、四川省の成都にまで拡大した（199)

これらのデモの一部には、改革・開放政策による国内の格差の拡大への不満など中国政府への不満も含まれていた（199)

その後も、中国への説明を続けたが、中国政府からは理解を得られず、中曽根は翌年の靖国神社参拝を断念した。以降、彼は首相在任中に１度もの靖国神社を参拝しなかった（200)

中曽根が公式参拝を中断した背景には、彼が胡耀邦と非常に親しい関係にあり、中曽根が参拝することで中国国内での胡耀邦への影響に配慮したこと、さらにはA級戦犯の分しする試みがトン罪したこともあげられる。1978年にA級戦犯が靖国神社に合祀されたことが知らされると、国内外で大きな波紋が広がり、昭和天皇もそれまで続けていた靖国参拝を取りやめている（201)

1986年第二次教科書問題

1986年5月に日本を守る国民会議が編纂する高校歴史教科書『新編日本史」が教科書検定の最終段階を合格した。日本を守る会は、1981年に設立された保守系の言論・政治団体であり、『新編日本史』では、対外侵略を正当化したり、日本の戦争被害を強調する記述が多く、民族主義的色調の強いものであった。もちろん中国、韓国からの記述内容是正の要求がなされ、政府は教科書販売もとに申請の取り下げを要求した。しかし、政治介入による出版取りやめに対し文部省が強く抵抗した。

1986年の第二次教科書問題は、1982年の第一次教科書問題と比べて、深刻な外交問題に発展することなく、比較的早期に解決が図られた。その理由として、個人的に親しい関係にあった中曽根と胡が日中関係の悪化を望まず、互いに善処したことがあげられる（203）

1989年 天安門事件

天安門事件に対し、日本政府は侵略戦争という過去の経緯から、日中関係が他の先進国と中国の関係とは異なることを強調した。しかし、他の主要国が対中経済関係を制限する中経済政策ぉ実施しない日本に対しては、人権よりもビジネスを重視しているとの批判が高まった。その結果、日本政府も中国への借款の供与や閣僚レベルの対中接触の停止を発表した。（227)

宮下明聡『ハンドブック　戦後日本外交史　対日講話から密約問題まで』ミネルヴァ書房、2017

[South Korea]

The 1980s began with military intervention in South Korean politics, the setback of democratization, and the dawn of a new Japan–Korea era under the renewed Cold War. However, over the course of the decade, democratization was achieved in South Korea, and the Cold War came to an end at the international level. Against this backdrop, Japan–Korea relations began to shift from an era in which both governments could fully control the relationship, to one in which growing exchanges in civil society began to influence bilateral relations (p. 169).

The 1980s was a period when the Cold War was moving toward an end at the international level, and the government-led structure of Japan–Korea relations also began to change (p. 170).

During this time, the channels of dialogue between Japan and South Korea diversified. Venues for structured dialogue that did not rely solely on personal ties between political leaders were gradually institutionalized. The Japan–Korea Regular Ministerial Conference, which had been held since 1967, was interrupted by political upheaval in South Korea in the late 1970s, but resumed in 1981 and has since been held annually (p. 170).

Parliamentary exchanges, which had previously served a strong complementary role to government relations, also began to diversify with the growth of opposition parties in democratizing South Korea and a shift in the stance of Japan’s left-wing parties (p. 170).

[South Korea]

Toward a New Japan-Korea Relationship

In the early 1980s, Japan-Korea relations took a new step forward, made possible by a change in South Korea’s leadership and a reshaping of Japan-Korea political networks. However, Prime Minister Nakasone’s official visit to Yasukuni Shrine in 1985 provoked concern and backlash from South Korea.

By the latter half of the 1980s, the international environment and Japan-Korea economic relations were evolving, but the most significant change was the impact of South Korea’s democratization.

In Japan during the mid-1980s, public interest expanded beyond South Korean political developments to include Korean culture and everyday life. Under Chun Doo-hwan’s government, a new attitude toward Japan was promoted under the slogan “Overcoming Japan” (克日), which differed from traditional “anti-Japanese” sentiment. This phrase spread especially after the 1982 First Textbook Issue.

Ahead of the Seoul Olympics, a Korean boom arose in Japan, drawing attention to Korean travel, cuisine, and entertainment. It was around this time that kimchi began to be widely adopted in Japanese food culture (p. 156).

More than twenty years after the normalization of diplomatic relations with South Korea, growing interest was now being directed toward Korean culture and daily life (p. 156).

First Textbook Issue (June 1982)

In November 1982, Japan introduced the “Neighboring Countries Clause” into the textbook screening guidelines. The clause stipulated that “consideration should be given to the treatment of modern and contemporary events involving neighboring Asian countries from the standpoint of international understanding and cooperation.” This was aimed at preventing the recurrence of textbook-related problems.

Second Textbook Issue (1986)

In dealing with the second textbook controversy, the Japanese government took the position of clearly acknowledging Japan’s wartime responsibility and colonial rule toward neighboring Asian countries (p. 161).

This also forced the Japanese government to modify its previously ambiguous “double standard” regarding wartime responsibility (p. 161).

貿易摩擦

1980年代にはいると、米国はレーガン政権のもと、大規模減税や軍事予算の増大などによりもたらされた財政赤字と高金利政策により生じたドル高を受けて国内企業が競争力を失ったことに起因する貿易赤字のいわゆる「双子の赤字」を抱えた。

対日不均衡を是正するため、米国政府は具体的な数値目標を掲げながら、報復措置に訴える構えを見せたり、実際に報復措置を発動したりすることで、日本に情報を迫るようになった。

たとえば、1986年に締結された半導体協定は日本にダンピング輸出の監視と外国製半導体の対日輸入促進を義務付けたが、その際非公開のサイド ・レターにおいて日本政府は、日本市場における外国製半導体販売シェアが5年間で２０%に達することが可能であり、その実現を歓迎するとして具体的な数値に言及した。

80年代後半から90年代初頭にかけて、米国は個別品目の市場開放に加え、日本のマクロ経済政策や商慣行、貯蓄などのいわゆる「構造障壁」を問題しするようになった。1989年9月に始まった日米構造協議において、米国は公共投資の大幅な増額、量販店など大型店の出店を規制する大規模小売り店舗法の廃止、通関時間の短縮、独占禁止法の改正、株式所有制限の強化などを日本に要求した。

米国側がこうした構造問題を取り上げるようになった背景には、円高・ドル安を誘導した1985年のプラザ合意以後も米国の対日貿易収支が改善しないことや、他の先進国の市場では高い競争力のある特定の米国製品が、日本市場では同等のシェアを獲得できないことなどに対するフラストレーションがあった
