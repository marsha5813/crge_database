Japan: Context (1920-1930)

Governmental Regulations on Religion and Legal Cases

During this period, there were still no specific restrictions on religion. Still, the scope of application of the less majesty(不敬罪, 1880）and police punishment (警察犯処罰令, 1908) was expanded to include policing and suppression of faith. In particular, the police punishment decree was easy to invoke, and new religions that were regarded by the police authorities as “licentiousness” were often restricted from expanding their organizations through frequent activities. On the other hand, the crime of impiety was first applied in the first Oomoto suppression. However, caution was required in its invocation to control the social spread and large-scale growth of new religions with doctrines that conflicted with the national constitution. (Sakamoto, 477)　Furthermore, in 1925, Peace Preservation Law was enacted. This was intended to punish the organization and the joining of associations whose purpose was to transform the state and deny the private property system. In practice, however, it functioned broadly as a sweeping crackdown on freedom of expression.(18)

1925 Peace Preservation Law （治安維持法, Chian iji hō) ・ It was enacted to crack down on socialist and labor movements. Under this law, it prohibited any social activities which rejected the emperor-centered political system and private property. In 1928, the death penalty was added as a maximum sentence, and the law was also manipulated to suppress the labor disputes, the democratic protests, and religious organizations. During this period, there were still no specific restrictions on religion. Still, the scope of application of the decree of impiety and police punishment was expanded to include policing and suppression of faith.

Peace Preservation Law, 1925

  1. Anyone who forms, or knowingly participates in, groups whose goal is to deny the system of private property or to change our national essence shall be sentenced to prison or penal servitude of up to ten years. Anyone who attempts to commit this crime also will be punished.

Social Hostility toward Christianity

After the death of founder Nao Deguchi in 1918, Oninsaburo Deguchi became the center of the organization, and from around that time onward, it expanded throughout Japan. In addition, people related to the navy and the army joined the Omoto, and their activities attracted the attention of the judicial authorities. In 1919, investigations began into the Imperial Way of Oomoto-kyo (495) The Kyoto Prefectural Police sent in police to gather information, and on February 11, 1921, Onizaburo and three others were arrested. At the trial, the charges against Deguchi Oninsaburo and Asano Kazaburo included violations of the Penal Code for disrespect and the Newspaper Law. The charges against Deguchi and Asano were enumerated. In the section on disrespect, it was stated that they had committed an act of gross desecration and disrespect of the Emperor’s dignity by writing articles in a newspaper published by the Omoto that delusionally commented on the Emperor’s actions, violated the wisdom of the Emperor, desecrated his dignity, and ignored his right to rule. Furthermore, publishing such an article violated the Newspaper Law. (496)

1928 First Suppression of Honmichi(ほんみち第一次弾圧)

This incident, known as the First Tenrikyo Research Association Incident, became an issue of disrespect. The Tenri Kenkyukai, which was started by Aijiro Onishi, a Tenrikyo believer, based on his interpretation, became an issue of disrespect to the emperor, and arrests continued from April 8 to August 1928. Almost 180 people were prosecuted, and 287 were exempted from prosecution. (496).

One opportunity to consider this period regarding the relationship between Japan and U.S. Christianity is the enactment of the Immigration Act of 1924 and also anti-American sentiment among the Japanese. The law went into effect on July 1, 1924, but in June, a declaration opposing the U.S. immigration law was issued by the Japanese Christian League.When the Immigration Act of 1924 was proposed, Some of Christians in Tokyo gathered at Shigenobu Okuma’s house and sent a telegram to the President of the United States to stop this Act, pointing out that it would harm US-Japan relations(Sunakawa, 1965, vol.2, 202).

After the Immigrant Act passage, Christians condemned it as the most severe violation of the spirit of Christianity, and on June 27, they issued a resolution jointly signed by them（Sunakawa, 1965,vol.2, 203)

After the passage of this Immigrant Act, Inazo Nitobe, who had studied in the United States and was a pro-American Christian, stated that he would “never step on American soil again” (Sawada, 2019, 133). Many other Christians were disappointed by the passage of the Act and were concerned about the deterioration of relations with the United States.

On the other hand, in response to such anti-American sentiment in Japan, activities were developed to solve the tension between the two countries through private cultural exchanges. It was called Friendship Dolls(Japanese friendship dolls (友情人形, yūjō ningyō), or Japanese ambassador dolls, and the American blue-eyed dolls (青い目の人形, aoi me no ningyō). About 12,000 dolls were donated from the United States in time for the Japanese Doll Festival. The movement was planned by Sydney Lewis Gulick (1860-1945), a missionary of the American Board who worked in Japan for many years. In 1925 the Committee of World Friendship among Children (CWFC) was formed. The first project of the CWFC was a goodwill activity through dolls during the Doll Festival on March 3, 1927 (Kohiyama, 2005, 57). On the Japanese side, the Ministry of Education and Foreign Affairs distributed the dolls, and the Shibusawa Foundation covered most of the expenses after Eiichi Shibusawa endorsed the project. On the other hand, 58 Japanese dolls were presented to the United States in November as “reply dolls” led by Shibusawa. They were donated to museums and art galleries throughout the United States.IIt was mentioned that many Christian women and their traditions behind the doll exchange.(Kohiyama, 2005, 65) Since the 19th century, many American women have engaged in overseas missionary work. As an extension, those who had previously participated in such missions created and prepared doll clothing (Kohiyama, 2005,61). Thus, it is shown that American Christian women supported this activity. On the other hand, Gulick and the CWFC were careful not to indicate too much that their activities were religious. They thought that it was essential to appeal to secular groups to receive support from the wider public (Kohiyama, 2005,62). Therefore, prioritizing the dolls occurred over appeals to ease US-Japan relations, such as “friendship,” rather than religious connotations(Kohiyama, 2005, 63-64). Thus, the project forgot about American women and the religion behind it. It was carried out to improve US-Japan relations and benefit the country, which was the concern of Japanese male leaders(Kohiyama, 2005, 66) . Furthermore, the project was also implemented in the colonies and used to reinforce Japanese colonial policies.

Geopolitical Dynamics and International Relationships

A. and C (Japanese; GPT translate

The 1920s to 1930s is often referred to as the Versailles–Washington system — an era characterized by diplomatic cooperation and international collaboration among Japan, the United States, and Great Britain.

In the aftermath of the First World War, a new international order was sought, and in pursuit of collective security, the League of Nations was established in 1920. While the United States had taken the lead in promoting the formation of the League, it ultimately refrained from joining due to opposition in the Senate. Japan, having entered the First World War on the side of the Allies, joined the League of Nations as a permanent member of the Council. This achievement could be seen as a milestone for Japan, which since the Meiji era had aimed to catch up with the Western powers.

At the same time, however, Japan remained reluctant to support efforts to strengthen the collective security framework centered on the League of Nations — a stance likely rooted in concerns that the League might interfere with Japan’s “special interests” in East Asia (Tomoaki Murakami, 2016, pp. 160–161). In particular, it was at the Washington Conference that the international order of the 1920s was effectively determined.

The Great Depression of 1929 led the world toward economic bloc-formation, and the moment when individual nations began to pursue narrow self-interest above international cooperation marked the collapse of the Versailles–Washington system that had supported the 1920s, bringing that era to a close.

1920-30年代はヴェルサイユ＝ワシントン体制と言われ、日本、アメリカ、イギリスの三カ国による協調外交、国際協調の時代であった。

第一世界大戦終結後、新たな国際秩序が模索され、集団安全保障の模索から1920年に国際連盟が設立された。国際連盟の結成をリードしたのはアメリカ合衆国であったが、上院の反対により参加を見送ることとなった。連合国側として第一次世界大戦に参戦した日本は、常任理事国として国際連盟に参加した。このことは明治以来、西洋列強に追いつくことを目標としてきた日本にとっては一つの到達点であったと言える。一方で、日本は国際連盟を中心とした集団安全保障体制を強化する取り組みに対しては消極的であり、その背景には東アジアにおける日本の特殊利益に国際連盟が介入することを危惧したものと考えられる（Tomoaki Murakami, 2016,160-161)　特にワシントン会議において1920年代の国際秩序体制が決定された。1929年の大恐慌は世界をブロック経済化へと向かわせ、その結果として各国が狭い自己の利益のみを追求する姿勢に徹した瞬間、1920年代を支えたヴェルサイユ＝ワシントン体制は崩壊し、一つの時代に終わりを告げた。

【Washington Naval Conference】

The most important conference that determined the international system of this era was the Washington Conference. This was an international conference held under the leadership of the United States, which was not a member of the League of Nations, from November 1921 to February 1922. The main agenda items were naval disarmament, Pacific issues, and Far Eastern issues. In addition to the United States, Japan, China, Britain, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Belgium, and Portugal participated.

During this conference, the Four-Power Treaty, Five-Power Treaty, and Nine-Power Treaty were concluded.

First, in December 1921, the Four-Power Treaty was signed by Japan, the United States, Britain, and France. Japan’s mandate over the South Pacific Islands under the Treaty of Versailles, along with the Anglo-Japanese Alliance, posed concerns for the United States, which was seeking to expand its influence in the Pacific region. As a result, the United States aimed for the dissolution of the Anglo-Japanese Alliance and instead pursued the Four-Power Treaty, which stipulated mutual respect for Pacific territories and the resolution of disputes through international conferences (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, pp. 171–172).

Furthermore, the Five-Power Treaty (Washington Naval Treaty) stipulated a ten-year suspension of the construction of capital ships (battleships and aircraft carriers), setting the following naval strength ratio: Britain and the United States at 5 each, Japan at 3, and France and Italy at 1.67 each. Japan accepted this treaty in view of financial burdens and the need for cooperation with the United States. In exchange, Japan proposed a ban on strengthening naval bases in Pacific islands, which was approved as Article 19. Britain scrapped the largest number of warships under this treaty, contributing to a decline in British influence. The naval disarmament treaty temporarily curbed the battleship-building race that had continued since World War I (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, p. 171).

Finally, in February 1922, the Nine-Power Treaty was signed by all participating countries. This treaty concerned China and confirmed respect for China’s sovereignty, territorial integrity, open-door policy, and equal opportunity (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, p. 172). Additionally, Japan was required to return former German rights and interests in Shandong Province, acquired through the Twenty-One Demands. Behind this treaty were American and British intentions to restrain Japanese expansion in China. Japan, from the perspective of international cooperation, agreed to return these former German rights and interests in Shandong Province (Jiaozhou Bay). This treaty curtailed Japanese expansion in China and revived negotiations between Japan and China (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, p. 173). This Washington system remained in place until Japan launched the Manchurian Incident in 1931.

　この時代の国際社会の体制を決めた最も重要な会議としてワシントン会議があげられる。これは1921年11月から1922年2月まで国際連盟に不参加のアメリカ合衆国主導で開催された国際会議である。主な議題は、海軍軍縮、太平洋問題、極東問題であり、アメリカの他に日本、中国、イギリス、フランス、イタリア、オランダ、ベルギー、ポルトガルが参加した。

その中で、4カ国条約、五カ国、9カ国条約がそれぞれ締結された。

　まず1921年12月に、日本、アメリカ、イギリス、フランスによる4カ国条約が締結された。ヴェルサイユ条約により南洋諸島を委任統治する日本、そして日英同盟の存在は、太平洋地域への進出を試みるアメリカにとって、警戒しなければならない問題であった。そのためアメリカは日英同盟の破棄を目指し、代わる条約として太平洋における相互理解の尊重や、国際会議による紛争の解決などを明記した4カ国条約の締結に至った（Ryuji Hattori, 2016, 171-172)

　さらに、五カ国条約（ワシントン海軍軍縮条約）では、各国の主力艦（戦艦と空母）建造を10年間停止し、各国の保有比率をイギリスとアメリカが各５、日本3、フランスとイタリアが各1.67と取り決めた条約であった。日本側は財政負担や対米協調の観点からこの条約を受諾し、その代わりに、太平洋の島々における海軍基地の強化禁止を提案し、第19条として承認された。この条約において最も多くの軍艦を廃棄したのはイギリスであり、このこともイギリスの影響力の低下につながった。この海軍軍縮条約により第一次世界大戦から続く戦艦建造の競争は一時的に抑えられた。(Ryuji Hattori, 2016, 171 )　最後に、1922年２月に全参加国による９カ国条約が締結された。これは中国に関する条約であり、中国の主権の尊重、領土保全、門戸開放と機会均等が確認された（Ryuji Hattori, 2016, 172 ）。さらに日本が２１カ条の要求で得た山東省の旧ドイツ権益などの返還が求められた。この条約締結の背景には、アメリカとイギリスが日本の中国進出を抑制する目的があり、日本も国際協調の観点から山東省の旧ドイツ権益（膠州湾）の返還を認めた。この条約により日本の中国への進出が抑制され、日本と中国の交渉も復活した（Ryuji Hattori, 2016, 173)このようなワシントン体制は、１９３１年に日本が満州事変を起こすまで続いた体制であった。

【United States of America】

In the 1920s, Japan placed great importance on international cooperation with the United States, which had emerged as the new center of global power, replacing Britain. In foreign policy, Japan’s approach was based on the Washington system and aimed to build friendly relations with the United States; however, issues such as the Immigration Act excluding Japanese and disputes over the Pacific region made relations with the U.S. highly sensitive.

One example is the Yap Island dispute, which took place between 1920 and 1923. Following the Paris Peace Treaty, Japan received a mandate to govern the former German territories in the South Pacific, but the U.S. was displeased with Japan’s mandate over Yap Island in particular. Yap Island was a key hub for undersea telegraph cables, and the two countries negotiated over its control. In February 1922, the U.S.-Japan Treaty on the Mandate for Yap Island was concluded (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, pp. 168–169). In return for American recognition of Japan’s mandate, Japan agreed to allow the U.S. to land and operate undersea cables connected to Yap Island and abolished military administration in the South Pacific Islands. In April 1922, Japan established the South Seas Bureau (南洋庁) on Koror Island (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, p. 169).

During this period, relations between Japan and the United States gradually deteriorated, especially after the passage of the Immigration Act of 1924, which excluded Japanese immigrants. Anti-Japanese sentiment on the American West Coast had intensified after the Russo-Japanese War. A well-known example was the 1906 incident in San Francisco, where Japanese children were denied entry to public schools. In response, the U.S. federal government and Japan negotiated self-restrictions on immigration. However, in 1913 the California State Legislature enacted the Alien Land Law, which barred first-generation Japanese immigrants (Issei) from owning land. This prompted strong anti-American sentiment within Japan.

1920年代、イギリスに代わり世界の中心に躍り出たアメリカとの国際協調関係を日本は重視した。外交面ではワシントン体制を基調とし、アメリカとの友好的な関係の構築を目指した日本であったが排日移民法や太平洋地域の問題などアメリカとの関係は非常に重要なものでもあった。

　たとえば、1920~23年にかけて起こったヤップ島の問題は、その一例と言える。パリ講和条約を経て日本は旧ドイツ領の南洋諸島、なかでもヤップとの委任統治に対しアメリカは快く思わなかった。ヤップ島は海底電線の要所であり、この島をめぐり日本とアメリカの間で1922年2月委任統治の日米条約が可決された（Ryuji Hattor, 2016, 168－169）アメリカが日本の委任統治を承認する代わりに、日本は将来ヤップ島に接続される海底電線の陸揚げと運用をアメリカに許可し、さらに南洋諸島での軍政を廃止し、1922年4月には南洋庁をコロール島に設置した（Ryuji Hattori, 2016, 169)

　この時代、日米関係は1924年に可決された排日移民法の成立によって徐々に悪化していった。とくに西海岸側での日本人移民に対する排斥の風潮は日露戦争後に高まった。1906年サンフランシスコで公立学校への日本人児童の入学が拒否された事件は代表的である。その度に連邦政府と日本の間では移民を自主規制するなどの方策が取られていった。一方で、1913年にはカリフォルニア州議会が排日土地法を制定し、日系一世による土地所有ができなくなり、この問題に対し日本国内でも反米運動が起こった。

Against this background, in 1920 the second Alien Land Law was enacted once again. Furthermore, Japan’s Twenty-One Demands toward China drew widespread sympathy among American citizens for the Chinese, and this sentiment was projected onto the local Japanese immigrant community, further worsening Japan’s image in the United States. At that time, American society was heavily influenced by racism, and in the House of Representatives, the Immigration Act excluding Japanese was passed without sufficient debate. It was also approved by the traditionally more internationally minded U.S. Senate, thus bringing the law into effect (Minohara, 2016, pp. 178–179).

This law stipulated that Asians were ineligible for naturalization, effectively prohibiting their immigration. While this category of “Asians” primarily referred to Chinese and Japanese, Chinese laborers had already been banned from immigration under the 1882 Chinese Exclusion Act; therefore, the new law effectively targeted the Japanese, completely prohibiting further Japanese immigration (Minohara, 2016, p. 179).

For the Japanese, who had attained permanent membership in the League of Nations and achieved parity with Western powers, this law—placing them on the same level as other Asian peoples—was perceived as a national insult. Japanese public opinion temporarily turned strongly anti-American. Many elites, including pro-American intellectuals who had promoted Japan’s modernization, were deeply shocked. Christian figures such as Nitobe Inazō and Uchimura Kanzō were particularly dismayed and expressed strong opposition to the law.

On the other hand, Takagi Yasuyuki, a fellow Christian and professor at Imperial University, offered a more level-headed interpretation of the situation, warning that one should not fall into the delusion that Americans were collectively conspiring to humiliate Japan (Okamoto Shunpei, Aruga Sada, pp. 54–55).

Although the enactment of this law did not cause a formal diplomatic rupture between the Japanese and American governments, the undercurrent of anti-American sentiment remained, and in time, it was exploited by right-wing and militarist ideologies (Okamoto Shunpei, Aruga Sada, pp. 54–55).

Furthermore, the stock market crash on October 24, 1929 in New York triggered a rapid downturn in the American economy and had a profound impact on the world economy as well (Satoshi Hattori, 2016, pp. 196–197). In response, the United States and other countries prioritized the recovery of their own national economies, which led to the collapse of the previously existing international cooperation framework (Satoshi Hattori, 2016, p. 197).

　このような背景がある中で、1920年に再び第二次排日土地法が制定された。さらに中国に対する21か条の要求は、米国市民の多くが中国に同情を寄せ、その反射として日本のイメージを現地の日系人に重ね、その印象はより悪化した。当時の米国社会では、人種主義の影響が強く、下院院議会では十分な議論を経ずに排日移民法は可決し、伝統的に対外関係を重んじた連邦乗員議会においても可決され、ここに排日移民法が成立した。（Minohara, 2016, 178－179）この法律では、アジア系は「帰化の資格を有志ない」人種と規定されていたため移民としての入国はできなくなった。なかでもここでのアジア系とは中国人と日本人であったが、1882年の法律によりすでに中国からの労働者の移民は禁止されていたため、この法律は日本人が対象となり、この規定により日本からの移民は完全に禁止された（Minohara, 2016, 179）。日本人にとっては国際連盟の常任理事国となり、西洋諸国と同等の地位を築いたにも関わらず、他のアジア人と同等の扱いを受けたということで日本という国家に対する侮辱として受け止められ、一時日本の世論は反米の風潮が強かった。特に日本の近代化を提唱した知米派を含めた多くのエリートが衝撃を受け、クリスチャンの新渡戸稲造や内村鑑三がこの法律の制定に対し大きなショックを受けて反発した。一方で同じくクリスチャンで帝国大学教授の高木八尺はアメリカの状況を冷静に解説し、アメリカ人が一体となって日本を侮辱しようとしていると考える妄想に陥るべきではないと述べた（岡本駿平、有賀貞、54－55）この法律によって日米両国政府間で関係が悪化するはなかったが、この反米感情は底流としてのこりやがて右翼や軍国主義の思想に利用されていった。（岡本駿平、有賀貞、54－55）

　さらに1929年10月24日のニューヨーク株式市場の株価暴落は米国経済を急激に後退させ、世界経済へも大きな影響を与えることとなった（Satoshi Hattori, 2016, 196－197）アメリカを始め各国は自国経済の立て直しを優先したためそれまでの国際協調体制の解体へと至った（Satoshi Hattori, 2016, 197）

【Soviet Union】

Relations between the Soviet Union and Japan had remained severed and hostile since the Siberian Intervention of 1918. Diplomatic relations between Japan and the Soviet Union were not restored until the signing of the Soviet–Japanese Basic Convention in 1925, and during the seven intervening years tensions between the two nations continued. From the 1920s onward, the Soviet Union established diplomatic relations with European countries, developed close commercial ties with the United States, and steadily enhanced its international standing (Naraoka, 2016, p. 182).

In Japan as well, there were voices calling for the establishment of diplomatic ties with the Soviet Union. Although the Japanese government conducted repeated negotiations, relations were not normalized. In addition to issues stemming from the Siberian Intervention, disputes over natural resources—particularly oil and fisheries—also existed between the two nations. The Japanese side insisted on securing oil fields as an absolute condition for withdrawal from northern Sakhalin, and this became a key issue for both parties, especially from an economic standpoint (Naraoka, 2016, p. 182).

Against this backdrop, the Beijing Conference began in September 1923. Initially, Japan had presented excessive demands, but following a change in government, Japan withdrew these excessive demands, expressed condolences over the Nikolaevsk Incident, and reduced its minimum request concerning oil interests in northern Sakhalin to a 55-year lease, thereby seeking to conclude the negotiations. On the other side, the Soviet Union also adopted a flexible stance and basically accepted Japan’s conditions. The Soviet–Japanese Basic Convention was concluded on January 20, 1925, ratified on February 25, and entered into effect (Naraoka, 2016, p. 183).

Since the Siberian Intervention, Japan and the Soviet Union had remained without diplomatic ties, but the Kato Takaaki Cabinet at the time suppressed domestic hardline and emotional opposition toward the Soviet Union, and worked to resolve the issue through careful management of information. The key factor driving this policy was a sense of crisis in Japan over the Soviet Union’s rising international status, as well as the pragmatic need to secure natural resources (Naraoka, 2016, pp. 183–184).

In addition, the Soviet Union had not participated in the Washington Conference and was excluded from the Washington system. While Japan had prevented the United States from strengthening its control over the Chinese Eastern Railway, it ultimately recognized joint management of the railway by China and the Soviet Union. This succeeded in blocking American expansion, but in return gave the Soviet Union a strategic foothold for its East Asia policy (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, p. 174).

ソ連と日本の関係は1918年のシベリア出兵以来、国交を断絶した敵対状態が続いていた。日本とソ連の国交が回復するのは、1925年の日ソ基本条約（Soviet–Japanese Basic Convention）においてであり、それまでの7年間は日本とソ連の間には緊張関係が続いた。1920年代以降、ソ連はヨーロッパの国々と国交を樹立し、アメリカとも通商関係において緊密な関係を築き、国際的な地位を向上させていた(Naraoka, 2016, 182)。一方、日本国内でもソ連との国交樹立を望む声があり、政府は交渉を重ねるも、国交の樹立には至らなかった。日本とソ連との間には、シベリア出兵の問題以外にも、石油や漁業を中心とする天然資源の問題が存在していた。日本側は北樺太からの撤兵の絶対条件として油田の確保をあげており、ソ連としても経済的な観点から両者の間で課題となっていた（Naraoka, 2016, 182）。

　このような中で1923年9月から北京会議が開始された。当初は過大な要求を突きつけた日本出会った政権が交代したこともありソ連に対し、過大な要求の撤回と尼港事件に対する哀悼の意を表明し、北樺太の石油利権に関しても55年という最低限の要求にとどめ交渉の締結を試みた。一方、ソ連側も日本側の要求を基本的に受け入れる柔軟な態度を示し、1925年1月20日に日ソ基本条約が締結され、2月25日に批准、発行に至った（Naraoka, 2016, 183 )

　シベリア出兵以降、日本とソ連は国交を断絶した状態であったが、当時の加藤高明内閣はソ連に対する国内の強硬論や感情論を抑え込み、情報を整理することで問題の解決に当たった。なかでも最大の要因となったのは、ソ連の国際的地位の向上に対する日本側の危機感と天然資源の確保という実利的な必要性に迫られてのことであった（Naraoka, 2016, 183－184）

さらにソ連はワシントン会議に参加しておらず、ワシントン体制からも排除されていた。日本はアメリカによる東支那鉄道管理体制の強化を阻止していたため、中国とソ連にその共同管理体制となることを認めた。このことは米国の進出を防いだ代わりに、ソヴィエトに東アジア政策の拠点を与える代償となった（Ryuji Hattori, 2016, 174）

【England】

After the First World War, Britain was burdened with enormous war debts, which diminished its influence in the international economy. This relative decline of British influence in East Asia allowed the United States to rise as a new global power.

　第一次世界大戦以降、イギリスは膨大な戦時債務を負担することとなり、国際経済における影響力を低下させることとなった。このことは東アジアにおけるイギリスの影響力の低下を相対的に示し、代わりに米国が新たな国家として台頭してきた。　

【China】

Although President Wilson’s principle of national self-determination was not applied to East Asia, it served as a catalyst for the May Fourth Movement in China. On the other hand, under the Washington system, Japan adopted a policy of non-intervention in China’s domestic affairs from the standpoint of international cooperation. Within the Washington framework, China occupied a subordinate position in international diplomacy led by Japan, Britain, and the United States.

In October 1925, based on the China Tariff Treaty concluded at the Washington Conference, the Beijing Special Tariff Conference was convened by the Beijing government to restore China’s tariff autonomy. Japan took the initiative in expressing a positive stance toward recognizing China’s tariff autonomy. However, since Beijing fell into a state of anarchy during the conference, the talks were indefinitely suspended. This dealt a blow to the Washington system, making it difficult to advance China’s treaty revision diplomacy smoothly (Nakatani, 2016, p. 187).

Meanwhile, the National Revolutionary Army advanced northward in its campaign to unify China, but Japan maintained a policy of non-intervention, which led to domestic criticism of its “weak diplomacy.” In 1927, under the newly formed Tanaka Giichi Cabinet, a more forceful policy relying on military strength was adopted, and the military’s influence began to grow (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, p. 190). Breaking from the previous non-interventionist approach, Japan decided to dispatch troops to Shandong Province for the purpose of protecting Japanese nationals, which forced the National Revolutionary Army to withdraw from the region. However, despite further negotiations, Chiang Kai-shek launched another northward campaign in April 1928. In response, Japan again dispatched troops to Shandong, resulting in a clash in Jinan in May (the Jinan Incident). Exaggerated reports of civilian damage by the Japanese side further escalated the fighting. The incident was eventually settled with the signing of the Jinan Incident Settlement Agreement in March 1929, though negotiations over its terms proved difficult (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, p. 190).

This incident is considered a turning point in international politics. One reason is that China’s anti-foreign movement, which had previously targeted Britain, now shifted its focus to Japan. Secondly, the incident worsened Chiang Kai-shek’s view of Japan. Thirdly, although Britain and the United States had initially cooperated with Japan’s first intervention in Shandong, they later grew critical of Japan as they moved closer to the Nationalist government. In particular, the United States formally established diplomatic relations with the Nationalist government in July 1928 and recognized China’s tariff autonomy (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, p. 191).

Furthermore, in May 1928, the assassination of Zhang Zuolin by Japanese forces occurred. The Tanaka Cabinet prioritized railway policy in Manchuria, and in 1927, through South Manchuria Railway Company President Yamamoto Jotaro, it had concluded an agreement with Zhang Zuolin, positioning relations with Zhang as a pillar of its Manchurian-Mongolian policy (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, p. 192). However, in June 1928, Zhang’s train was bombed and he was killed while traveling from Beijing to Fengtian (Shenyang). This was an act by the Kwantung Army, which caused the Tanaka Cabinet to lose Zhang, a key figure in its Manchurian policy (Ryuji Hattori, 2016, pp. 192–193). The assassination, unexpected by the Tanaka Cabinet, led to the stagnation of Japan’s railway policy in Manchuria and provoked domestic criticism of its foreign policy. As a result, in July 1929, the Tanaka Cabinet resigned en masse.

　ウィルソンによる民族自決主義の原則は東アジアには適用されなかったが中国における五・四運動のきっかけになった。一方、日本はワシントン体制のもと中国に対し国際協調の観点から、内政不干渉の姿勢をとっていた。ワシントン体制の中国は国際協調外交において日英米の従属的な地位に位置づけられていた。

　1925年10月ワシントン会議で締結された中国関税条約に基づき、北京政府によって中国の関税自主権の回復を目指す北京関税特別会議が開かれた。日本は率先して中国の関税主権の承認に前向きであることを表明したものの、会議中に北京が無政府状態となったため会議は無期休会となった。このことは、ワシントン体制にとっても痛手となり、中国の条約改正外交を円滑に勧めることが困難となった。(Nakatani, 2016, 187)　

　その間、国民革命軍は中国の統一を目指し北方へ進軍したが、日本はこれに関しても内政不干渉の姿勢を取り、日本国内からは「軟弱外交」と避難された。1927年日本は、田中義一内閣が成立し、軍事力に依拠した強硬策が取られるようになり、軍部の力も増加していった（Ryuji Hattori, 2016, 190）これまでの内政不干渉から居留民の保護を目的に山東省へ派兵が決定し、やがて国民革命軍は山東省から撤退した。しかし、その後の話し合いにも関わらず蒋介石は1928年4月再び北方への進軍を行うと、日本側も再び山東省への出兵を実施し、5月済南にて衝突した（済南事件）日本側が居留民の被害を誇大に報告したこともあり戦闘は拡大した。この事件は1929年3月に済南事件解決文章に調印し集結したが、この処理を巡っては交渉が難航した（Ryuji Hattori, 2016, 190）この事件は、国際政治の転換点となったと考えられる。その理由の一つは、それまでイギリスを敵としてきた中国の排外運動が日本を標的にとするように変化したこと、2つ目に済南事件は蒋介石らの対日観を悪化させたこと、3つ目に第一次山東出兵には英米両国も同調していたが、国民党に接近する立場から日本に批判的担っていったことがあげられる。特にアメリカは1928年7月に国民政府と正式な外交関係を樹立し、中国の関税自主権も認める関係となった（Ryuji Hattori, 2016,, 191）。

さらに1928年5月には日本軍による張作霖爆殺事件が発生する。田中内閣は満州における鉄道政策を重視しており、1927年満州鉄道の山本条太郎社長を介して、張作霖との協定を結び、張作霖との関係を外交政策の一つの柱として重視していた（Ryuji Hattori, 2016, 192)。しかしながら、1928年6月、北京から奉天へ向かう張作霖の列車が爆破され、殺される事件が発生した。関東軍による事件であり、これによって田中内閣は対蒙満政策の柱と位置づけてきた張作霖を失った(Ryuji Hattori, 2016, 192－193）。田中内閣にとって関東軍による張作霖殺害は予想外の事件であり、これにより、満州での鉄道政策は停滞し、国内からも外交政策に対する批判が起こり、1929年7月田中内閣は総辞職することとなった。

【Korea】

朝鮮においても同様で、ウィルソンの民族自決主義の原則は適用されなかったが、三・一独立運動のきっかけとなった。この時代、日本は植民地統治の方針として内地延長主義、同化主義を打ち出した。この方針は、台湾や朝鮮における統治は、「植民地支配ではない（＝内地の一部である）」ということを国際社会にアピールしようとしたものと考えることができる（Komagome, 279 )
